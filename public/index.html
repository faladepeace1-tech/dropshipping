What Is Dropshipping? A Simple Explanation

What Is Dropshipping? A Simple Explanation

What dropshipping really is - in simple English, no big grammar.

01

Someone buys something from your shop.

02

You send that order to another company (the supplier).

03

The supplier packs it and sends it straight to your customer.

04

You never see or touch the item yourself.

Your job is just to bring customers and sell. The supplier's job is to send the product.

How you make profit

You find a product that costs, say, $100 from the supplier. You sell it in your shop for $250. The supplier gets their $100, you keep the $150 difference. That's your profit. You don't pay for the product until someone actually buys it - so there's no risk of buying things that won't sell.

Founder, Nexatech

What dropshipping is, in plain English

Dropshipping is a retail model where you sell products without holding inventory. When a customer buys from your store, the order is sent to a supplier who packs and ships the item directly to that customer. You keep the difference between your selling price and the supplier cost. Because you do not pay for stock upfront, you can test products with less risk than a traditional shop.

The model still requires real work: choosing products people want, building trust, setting up payments and bringing visitors to the store. That is where a done-for-you service helps. We handle the store setup and supplier wiring so you can focus on marketing and customer service.
