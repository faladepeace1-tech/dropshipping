Dropshipping Store Setup Packages Explained

Every package is a done-for-you build. The difference is how many products you get, how much branding and creative work is included, and how long we support you after handover.

Starter - $149

Starter is for a first store with a focused product range. We set up one niche store on a premium theme, research 5 products, connect your supplier and fulfillment flow, integrate payments and give you 14 days of support. It is the leanest way to get a working store without building it yourself.

Pro - $299

Pro adds more products and more brand work. You get 10 researched products with ad angles, custom branding and logo support, abandoned-cart automation and 30 days of priority support. Choose Pro if you want a store that looks like a real brand from day one.

Elite - $599

Elite is for founders who want a broader catalog and more launch assets. It includes 20 products, 3 custom ad creatives, a 60-minute growth call and 60 days of support. It is the best fit if you plan to test paid traffic quickly after handover.

What is included in every package

  • Niche and product research before the build starts.
  • Premium store design with mobile-first pages.
  • Conversion-focused copy for the home and product pages.
  • Supplier connection through DSers or AutoDS.
  • Payment gateway integration and checkout testing.
  • Basic on-page SEO and a handover walkthrough.
  • 100% ownership of the store, domain and customer data.

What is not included

The package price does not include your domain, platform subscription, paid apps, product samples, ad spend or payment provider fees. We explain these before you buy so there are no surprises. We also do not guarantee sales or income.

Which package should you choose?

Choose Starter if you want a clean first store and a small product range. Choose Pro if branding and a larger catalog matter. Choose Elite if you want more products, creative assets and a longer support window. If you are unsure, send us a message and we will recommend a tier based on your budget and target market.
