Dropshipping Store Setup FAQ

Dropshipping Store Setup FAQ: 40 Clear Answers

Everything we get asked before a build: pricing, timeline, suppliers, payments, ownership, support and what happens after launch. If your question is not here, send us a message or chat on WhatsApp.

Quick answer: Nexatech builds done-for-you dropshipping stores in 7 to 14 days. Packages start at $149, you own the store 100%, and we connect products, suppliers and payments before handover.

Getting started

1. What is a done-for-you dropshipping store?
It is a store that our team researches, designs, builds and connects for you. You receive a launched store with products, supplier links, payment settings and a handover walkthrough instead of an empty template.
2. Do I need experience to start?
No. Most of our clients are first-time founders. We handle the technical setup and explain how to process orders and manage the store after handover.
3. What do you need from me to start?
Your preferred niche or product direction, brand name ideas, target country, contact details and access to the store account you want us to build in.
4. Can you choose the niche for me?
Yes. If you do not have a niche, we research options based on demand, competition and supplier availability, then recommend a direction before building.
5. How many products are included?
Starter includes 5 researched products, Pro includes 10 and Elite includes 20. Product count is listed on the pricing page for each tier.
6. Can I use my own logo and brand colors?
Yes. Send your logo, colors and any brand guidelines. If you do not have them, Pro and Elite include branding support.
7. Do you write the product descriptions?
Yes. The build includes conversion-focused copy for the home page, product pages and key trust sections. We do not copy supplier descriptions word for word.
8. Can you migrate my existing store?
Yes, migration is available as a custom scope. Send us the current platform, product count and what you want to keep so we can quote it.

Pricing and payment

9. How much does a Nexatech store cost?
Current packages are Starter at $149, Pro at $299 and Elite at $599. The exact deliverables for each tier are listed on the pricing page.
10. Are there hidden fees?
No hidden build fees. You pay the package price shown at checkout. Separate costs such as domain, Shopify subscription, apps or ad spend are explained before you buy.
11. Do you offer payment plans?
Payment plans are considered case by case for larger projects. Ask before checkout and we will confirm what is available.
12. What payment methods do you accept?
Checkout supports the payment options shown on the checkout page for your country and currency, including card and supported crypto options where available.
13. Can I pay in my local currency?
The storefront displays supported currencies and converts from USD at checkout. The final charge is shown before you confirm payment.
14. Can I upgrade my package later?
Yes. You can move to a higher tier before or after handover. We quote the difference based on the work already completed.
15. What happens after I pay?
You receive an order confirmation and a kickoff message. We collect your brand details, confirm the niche and start the build within the agreed window.
16. Is my payment and data secure?
Payments are processed by the checkout provider, not stored on our servers. We use encrypted connections and restrict admin access. Never send card details through chat.

Build and timeline

17. How long does the build take?
Most builds take 7 to 14 days after kickoff. The timeline depends on the package and how quickly you provide brand details and approve the direction.
18. Which platform do you build on?
Most builds use Shopify because it supports reliable payments, supplier apps and mobile performance. We confirm the platform before kickoff.
19. Will the store work on mobile?
Yes. Every build is mobile-first because most dropshipping traffic comes from phones. We test the key pages on common screen sizes before handover.
20. Do you set up taxes and shipping rules?
We configure the basic shipping zones, rates and tax settings relevant to your target market. Complex multi-country tax compliance may require a local accountant.
21. Do you set up payments?
Yes. We connect the payment gateway available for your country and test the checkout flow. Approval for the gateway account is controlled by the provider, not by us.
22. Why was my payment gateway rejected?
Gateways review business type, country, ID and risk. We help you prepare the application, but the final decision belongs to the provider. We can suggest alternatives if one is declined.
23. Is SEO included?
Basic on-page SEO is included: titles, descriptions, headings, image alt text and clean URLs. Ongoing content and link building are separate services.
24. What if I am not happy with the design?
You get revision rounds during the build. Tell us what feels wrong and we adjust within the agreed scope. Major direction changes after approval may be quoted separately.

Suppliers and fulfillment

25. Do you connect suppliers?
Yes. We source and connect suppliers through tools such as DSers or AutoDS, configure shipping profiles and test the order flow before handover.
26. What shipping times should I expect?
Shipping depends on the supplier and destination. We prioritize suppliers with reasonable delivery windows and show realistic estimates on the store instead of promising next-day delivery.
27. Can you find suppliers in my country?
Where possible, yes. Local or regional suppliers can reduce delivery time. If none are suitable, we explain the international options and their trade-offs.
28. What if a supplier runs out of stock?
We set up backup suppliers where available and show you how to pause or replace a product. Supplier stock changes are a normal part of dropshipping.
29. Do you handle fulfillment after launch?
The build includes fulfillment automation, not ongoing order processing. You can process orders yourself using the walkthrough or ask for a separate management quote.
30. Can I add more products later?
Yes. The store is yours, so you can add products at any time. We also show you how to research and import new products during handover.

Ownership, support and refunds

31. Do I own the store and domain?
Yes. The store is built in your account and the domain is registered in your name. You keep full ownership of the store, content and customer data.
32. Do you guarantee sales?
No. Anyone who guarantees sales is not being honest. We build the store, connect the tools and give you a launch plan, but results depend on your offer, traffic and follow-through.
33. What support is included?
Starter includes 14 days of support, Pro includes 30 days and Elite includes 60 days. Support covers build-related questions and fixes within the agreed scope.
34. What is your refund policy?
Because the work is done for you, deposits are non-refundable. We keep revising until the agreed handover criteria are met. Full terms are on the terms page.
35. Can I cancel after paying?
Cancellation depends on how much work has started. Contact us immediately if your situation changes so we can review the project status and terms.
36. Can I see examples of your work?
Yes. The portfolio section on the home page shows stores we have built. You can also ask for a walkthrough during a private chat.
37. Are the reviews real?
We only publish reviews and proof uploaded by clients or pulled from our database. If a section is empty, we hide it rather than fill it with invented testimonials.
38. Do you provide product images and videos?
Product media comes from the supplier and licensed sources. Elite includes custom ad creatives. We do not use another brand's copyrighted assets.

Ads, growth and next steps

39. Do you run ads for me?
We set up your pixel and first campaign structure and give you a scaling playbook. Ongoing media buying is available separately through mentorship.
40. How much ad budget do I need?
There is no single number that fits every market. A realistic starting budget should cover enough testing to gather data without risking money you cannot afford to lose.
